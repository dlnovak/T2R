Chinese Checkers

New game

Race all ten of your marbles across the board into the opposite point.

Players
Seats
How to play
  • On your turn, move one marble: step into an adjacent empty hole, or hop over any single adjacent marble (anyone's) into the empty hole right behind it.
  • Hops can be chained in one turn as long as each lands in an empty hole. Tap a marble to see every place it can reach.
  • First to fill the opposite point wins. If an opponent squats in your point, you still win once it's full and holds at least one of yours.
