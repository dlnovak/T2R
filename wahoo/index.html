      New game

      Pick at least two players.

      How to play
      • Roll a 1 or 6 to move a marble from your base strip onto your colored start square.
      • Marbles travel clockwise, out and back around every arm. Rolling a 6 earns another roll.
      • You can never jump or land on your own marbles. Jump rivals freely.
      • Land exactly on a rival to send it back to its base.
      • Shortcut: the six ringed holes near the middle are shortcut holes. The center hole counts as one space past any of them, so reach one with a pip to spare and the last pip drops you into the center. From the center, roll a 1 to pop out on the ringed hole just before your own arm.
      • After a full lap you turn down your arm's home strip. It needs an exact count. First to fill home wins.
      • Tap or hover a marble to see each hole numbered along its path.
